## When to draw one

A floor plan is usually uploaded: a picture, an SVG, a PDF or a DXF/DWG drawing. When there is none, open the
place (a building, a hall), go to **Indoors → Floor plans** and choose **draw a plan**.

## Drawing it

- Give the plan a **title**, the building's **width** and **depth** in metres and, if it has floors, the floor.
- **Add rooms**: drag on the plan to draw one, or **+ a room**. Drag a room to move it; the mouse moves by half a
  metre. Select a room to type its name, number and exact position and size (from the building's top-left corner).
- A room can **stand for a room record** of this place: choose it in the room, or add it with its **+ name** button.
  With **Place the room records at their room's centre**, saving puts each of those records at its room's centre,
  so whatever is inside the room shows where the room is.
- Rooms cannot overlap or go past the walls: the drawing says so before you save.

What you see while drawing is the plan as ARGUS will draw it: walls, a column grid every 5 m with its axes, each
room in the colour of what it is for, its name, number and area, doors, north and a scale bar. It is schematic,
not a survey.

## Afterwards

The plan is a plan like any other: place records on it, place it on the map, open it **with layers** or **in 3D**.

To change it, choose **edit drawing** on the plan. The building keeps its corner, so every record already placed on
the plan keeps its point; a building made smaller than what is on it is refused. An uploaded drawing cannot be
changed this way.
