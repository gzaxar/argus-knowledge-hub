An **activity** is a piece of work someone plans, is doing or has done: an inspection, a replacement, a check. It is
not a ticket: a ticket is a problem to fix or a request to someone; an activity records the work. An activity may
refer to an asset, a ticket or a document — and changes none of them: repairing a pump whose fault ticket stays
open is an activity about that ticket, and the ticket is left as it is.

## What an activity holds

- **Planned**: when it is planned (a start, an end or a due time). **Under way**: when it started. **Done**: when
  it was actually done — which is not when it was recorded: work done last week and recorded today is dated last
  week. **Skipped** or **cancelled**.
- **Who sees it**: the workspace (its members who may read its tickets), or **only its owner** and the person it
  is assigned to. An activity about a record you may not see is not shown to you — not in lists, counts or search.
- **Who changes it**: its owner and its assignee; for a workspace activity, also those who may change the
  workspace's tickets.
- **Which workspaces**: the one it was created in, and any other of **your** workspaces you add it to — a
  round that covers two facilities is one activity, listed and counted in both. In each workspace it follows
  that workspace's rules above. Change them with **Change…** (web) or **Change** (phone). It may leave the
  workspace it was created in only if it refers to no asset, ticket or document, because those records belong
  to that workspace. Someone who cannot see a record it refers to does not see the activity, in any workspace.
  A recurring activity's occurrences are in the series' workspaces, so the workspaces change for **this and
  future occurrences** or **the entire series**, not for one occurrence.

## Recurring activities

"Check the cooling system every Monday at 09:00": a **series** with a rule — every day, chosen weekdays, every
week, every month, every few days or weeks, until a date or a number of times. The rule keeps its local time in
its time zone: 09:00 stays 09:00 across the changes of daylight saving. Each occurrence is done, skipped or moved
on its own; changing one asks whether it is for **this occurrence**, **this and future occurrences** (the series
is split there) or **the entire series**. Work already done keeps the words it was done under. A series without an
end is never stored as endless rows: its occurrences are computed for the dates you look at.

## Capture by voice (phone)

**Capture by voice** on the home page, on an asset (for a fault on it, or work done on it) or in the menu:

1. Speak: what you say appears as text, which you can correct by typing. The phone turns speech into text; only the
   text goes to ARGUS, to propose a record; no audio is kept.
2. **Propose**: the assistant proposes a **ticket** (a fault, an incident, a request) or an **activity** (work
   planned, under way or done), says why, quotes the words it relied on and marks what it is unsure of. When it
   cannot tell — or a date could mean two days — it asks one question; answer it and it proposes again.
3. Check and correct the preview: title, description, the ticket's type (only types installed in the workspace),
   category and impact — never a priority, and *safety* only if you spoke of safety — or the activity's state,
   date and repetition. Dates are read from the moment you spoke, in your time zone: "yesterday", "now", "next
   Monday" are shown resolved. A part of the day ("Monday morning") is not an hour: none is made up; nor is a
   duration. Work is *done* only if you said it was done.
4. If an open ticket looks like the same matter, you can **add what you said to it** instead of a duplicate.
5. **Create** — only now is anything written. The result opens, and can be corrected.

Without the assistant, the text stays and you choose ticket or activity and fill the form. Offline, the capture is
kept on the phone as a draft, proposed when you open it again online; nothing is created from a guess. A confirmed
record waits in **Unsent changes** while ARGUS cannot be reached, and is sent once — never twice — when it can.

## The activity lists

The **cockpit** (home page, web and phone) lists the workspace's activities that need attention: those
**under way**, those **overdue** and those **planned for today**, with how many were **done today**.

On the phone, **Activities** (home page or menu): **Today**, **Upcoming** (30 days), **Completed** (the last 30
days) — yours; start or finish one with a tap. **Workspace**, on the phone and on the web (**Activities** in the side
bar): the activities of a range of dates, by **planned date** or by the **date the work was done**, as a
calendar or a list, with counts (planned, under way, done, overdue…) and filters (state, owner, asset, ticket,
recurring, private). An activity that starts before the range and runs into it is in it. Open one to see when it
was planned, done and recorded, where it came from — the words, for a voice capture — and to change or finish it.
