## The steps

In the ARGUS Field app, **Survey a rack** walks through a rack:

1. **The rack**: a rack already in ARGUS (find it), or one not recorded yet. Photograph its front, and its rear if
   useful. For a new rack say its name, its height in units and its room; choose its point on the floor plan only
   if you can see where it stands, and confirm it.
2. **Each unit**: **Photograph a unit**, and **Its label** when the serial number or model is on a separate label.
   What is read is *proposed*: each value says where it was read and how sure the reading is. Tap **Use** to take a
   value, or tap the value to type it. A value nobody could read stays *not read — type it*: nothing is invented.
3. **Which record**: *a record already in ARGUS*, *a new record*, or *leave it out*. If the unit may already be
   recorded — the same serial number, the same name — it is shown: **It is this one** links it; otherwise tick *I
   checked: it is another unit*. Tap a unit number on the rack drawing to say where it is.
4. **Review and save**: the list says what is still missing, which units overlap, which may be duplicates. When it
   is empty, **Save the rack**: the rack, the new records, their positions and the photos are saved together.

## Stopping and resuming

You can stop at any step: the survey is kept, and **Left half-way** brings it back, on any phone.
